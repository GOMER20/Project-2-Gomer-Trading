With real exchange hours the practice market opens and closes with the US stock market, on its trading days, and prices stand still in between. Around the clock is for practising or testing at any hour.

  1. Create a free account at Alpaca.
  2. In the Alpaca dashboard, choose Paper Trading, then API Keys → Generate.
  3. Paste both values here.

Your keys stay on this Mac, in Gomer Trading's data folder. They are used to read prices only. No order is ever sent to Alpaca.

US stocks trade 9:30 am to 4:00 pm New York time on weekdays. Outside those hours you will see the last session's prices.

Alpaca allows one live connection per account, so use these keys in one app at a time.
